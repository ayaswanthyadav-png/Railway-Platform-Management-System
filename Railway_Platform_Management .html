Problem Statement

Railway stations handle multiple trains arriving and departing from limited platforms. Incorrect or conflicting platform assignments can cause operational confusion for passengers.

Project Task

Create a platform management simulator for assigning trains to available platforms.

Modules of the Project

  1. Train schedule
  2. Platform availability
  3. Arrival / departure and platform assignment
  4. Conflict detection
  5. Station display

Key Terms

Platform
A raised area beside the track where passengers board or leave a train.
Assignment
Allotting one train to one platform from its arrival to its departure time.
Conflict
Two trains allotted to the same platform at overlapping times.

The PF number is shown on the station display. Always check the platform before boarding.

Conflict Detection

Two trains conflict when they are given the same platform and the arrival of one train comes before the departure of the other.

Conflict if:  arrival(B) < departure(A)
              and platform(A) = platform(B)
